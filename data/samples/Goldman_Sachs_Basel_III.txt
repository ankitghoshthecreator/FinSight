Under Basel III capital adequacy guidelines, Goldman Sachs reported a Supplementary Leverage Ratio (SLR) of 5.8% and a Tier 1 Risk-Based Capital Ratio of 16.7% for the current period. Common Equity Tier 1 (CET1) capital stood at $102.4 billion against Risk-Weighted Assets (RWAs) of $680 billion. The firm maintained a Liquidity Coverage Ratio (LCR) of 132%, well above the regulatory requirement of 100%. Total assets were $1.64 trillion, supported by strong global banking and wealth management net revenues of $12.7 billion. Net interest income was $1.61 billion for the quarter.
