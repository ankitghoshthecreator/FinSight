In the Federal Reserve Comprehensive Capital Analysis and Review (CCAR) supervisory severely adverse scenario, JPMorgan Chase demonstrated minimum post-stress Common Equity Tier 1 (CET1) capital ratio of 10.4%, significantly exceeding the regulatory threshold of 4.5%. Total loss absorbing capacity (TLAC) remained compliant at 24.8% of risk-weighted assets. Value at Risk (VaR) at 99% confidence interval averaged $42 million across fixed income and equity market operations. The Board of Governors authorized a share repurchase program of up to $30 billion alongside a quarterly common dividend increase to $1.25 per share.
